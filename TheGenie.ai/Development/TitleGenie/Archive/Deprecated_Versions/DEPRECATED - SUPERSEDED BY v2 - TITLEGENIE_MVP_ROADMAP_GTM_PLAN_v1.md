## Core Positioning

### The Big Idea

> **TitleGenie is a Knowledge Generation System for Title Reps.**
>
> **Knowledge is NOT regulated.**

### Positioning Statement

TitleGenie is NOT a farming platform. It's a **Title Business Development Platform** — built on analytics and intelligence to help title reps deliver knowledge that helps agents invest their marketing dollars where listings are trending.

### Compliance Model

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **No co-marketing** | Title reps don't pay for agent marketing |
| **No lead buying** | Title reps don't buy leads for agents |
| **No direct payments** | Title reps don't give products to agents |
| **Knowledge is allowed** | Analysis, insights, statistics = compliant |
| **1ParkPlace sponsors** | Free Paisley for agents — paid by 1ParkPlace, not title rep |

### If Department of Insurance Asks

**"What did the title rep give the agent?"**

Answer: **"Nothing. They referred them to a platform. 1ParkPlace/TheGenie gave them access to tools."**

## Target Customer

### Customer Type Hierarchy

| Priority | Customer Type | Description |
|----------|---------------|-------------|
| **1** | Title Companies | Enterprise accounts (First American, WFG, Fidelity, etc.) |
| **2** | Brokerages | Real estate brokerages using TitleGenie tools |
| **3** | Teams | Team leaders with agents underneath (not "Mega Teams") |
| **4** | Individual Agents | Solo agents |
| **5** | Affiliates | Non-title companies that buy data |
| **6** | Resellers | Website companies, etc. who resell products |
| **Future** | Competition Command | Separate category — personal network building |

### Title Rep Personas (Two Levels)

| Level | Persona | Comfort Zone | What They Use |
|-------|---------|--------------|---------------|
| **Level 1** | Mass Title Reps (Majority) | Giving away **CONTENT** | Reports, farming tools, content packages |
| **Level 2** | Power Title Reps (Minority) | Using **ANALYTICS** | Farm Analyzer, Agent Scorecard, data-driven conversations |

**Strategy:** Lead with content (Paisley), mobile-first. Analytics for those who level up.

## Pricing Model

### Simple Pricing

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Monthly Price** | **$250/month** |
| **Annual Price** | **$2,500/year** (2 months free) |
| **Included** | 4 Listing Commands/month ($400 value) |
| **Variable** | Number of agents allowed to invite (flexible — may start at 50) |

### ROI Story

| Title Rep Pays | Title Rep Gets | Break-Even |
|----------------|----------------|------------|
| $250/month | $400 worth of Listing Commands to gift | 1 title order ($500+ revenue) |

### Compliance Note on Listing Command Gifts

- **Who pays?** 1ParkPlace (not the title rep)
- **Who gives it?** TheGenie system (not the title rep)
- **Title rep's role:** Referral source — they recommended the agent
- **If DOI asks:** "I referred them to a platform. I didn't pay for anything."
