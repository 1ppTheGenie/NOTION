This document attempts to outline the approach and logistical coordination of roles and responsibilities for scaling the Title Rep Invite CEO to Zoom process
Synopsis:
Title Reps have a calendar link to schedule their large clients for a one on one strategy session with a Genine.ai expert.  The agent must have at leas 6 listings in the past 12 months and the title rep must be on the zoom.  

The meeting is designed to set the stage for free launch of 1 Text Message only Listing Command campaign for the agent.
